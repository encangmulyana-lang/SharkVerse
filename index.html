■ FAQ

SharkVerse is a school of 4,444 pixel-art sharks patrolling the BNB Chain. From the humble Reef Shark to the glowing Mythic Leviathan, each NFT is a retro 16-bit predator of the deep.

Your first mint per wallet is FREE — you only pay gas. After that the price climbs by how many YOU have minted: #2–#10 cost 0.00015 BNB each, #11–#20 cost 0.0004 BNB each, and #21–#30 cost 0.0008 BNB each.

Max 30 sharks per wallet, max 10 per transaction. The price ladder follows your wallet's own mint count, not the global supply.

Every mint is a random draw from the six tiers: Reef Shark, Hammerhead, Tiger Shark, Great White, Megalodon and the Mythic Leviathan (only 33 ever). Token #1 is the Mythic Leviathan.

The contract is deployed and the live count is already moving. Mint opens when we announce it — the button activates then. No wallet is needed to browse the site.
